Stringed instruments

A guitar is a . Some strings are thicker than others. Stringed instruments have pegs that you can use to the instrument by making the strings looser or tighter. When the guitar is tuned, the gives a perfect sound. If you listen to music and the instrument is out of tune the music sounds horrible.

You can also make the strings shorter by pressing them down. These actions change the pitch of the notes.

✅ Tighter string = higher-pitched note.

✅ Thicker string = lower-pitched note.

2. Are the vibrations faster or slower when you tighten a string?

✅ The vibrations are faster when you tighten a string!

3. Are the vibrations faster or slower when you pluck a thinner string?

✅ The vibrations are faster when you pluck a thinner string!
